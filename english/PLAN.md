# English Track — Plan & Method

**Daily minimum:** learn **one** term / phrase / grammar point and use it in your own sentence. Log it in `LOG.md`.
**What to pick:** `ROADMAP.md` → Track EN.
**Corrections & vocabulary:** `vocabulary.md` (only correct versions).

---

## Daily (10–15 min)
- Read one technical blog post (Go Blog, Cloudflare, Stripe Engineering)
- Note 2–3 new technical phrases in `vocabulary.md`
- Once per week: say your project description out loud in English

## Shadowing (3x/week, 10 min)
- Source: Go podcasts (Fallthrough, Cup o' Go), conference talks (GopherCon)
- Method: listen 30 sec → pause → repeat exactly, same rhythm and intonation
- Target: less hesitation when speaking under interview pressure

## Interview English — must-know phrases

| Situation | Phrase |
|---|---|
| Thinking | "Let me think through this..." / "Give me a moment to reason about this..." |
| Clarifying | "Before I answer, can I ask — what's the scale we're designing for?" |
| Don't know | "I haven't used that directly, but here's how I'd approach it..." |
| Trade-off | "The trade-off here is..." / "This approach gives us X, but costs us Y..." |
| Disagreeing | "That's a valid approach. My instinct would be X because..." |
| Stuck | "I'm thinking out loud here — one approach would be..." |
| Mistake | "Actually, let me revise that — I think the better answer is..." |

## Transfer errors to eliminate (Uzbek → English)
- **Articles:** "the database", "a complete roadmap" — always check a/an/the before nouns
- **Prepositions:** "depends **on**", "work **on** a project", "participate **in**", "write code **in** Go"
- **Tense:** past for finished work ("I built X") vs present perfect for experience ("I've worked with...")
- **Agreement:** "some skills **don't** matter" (plural subject → plural verb)
- **Word order:** adjective before noun ("slow query", not "query slow")
